8D WALL Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

8D WALL is a live wallpaper and device-personalization application. This Privacy Policy explains how the current version of the app handles information.

Information We Collect

The current version of 8D WALL does not require account registration and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or camera data.

Internet Access

The app declares Internet access so that online functionality can operate when required. The current app build does not include advertising or analytics SDKs such as Google AdMob or Firebase Analytics.

Device Sensors

8D WALL may use the device gyroscope, when available, to support visual wallpaper effects. Gyroscope access is optional and is used locally on the device for visual interaction. We do not use the gyroscope to identify you.

AI / On-device Processing

The app includes TensorFlow Lite components for on-device functionality. Processing performed through these components is designed to run locally on the device unless a future feature clearly states otherwise.

Advertising and Analytics

The current version of 8D WALL does not include third-party advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

Data Sharing

Because the current version does not intentionally collect personal data, we do not sell or share personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Children's Privacy

8D WALL is not designed to collect personal information from children. If future versions introduce features that collect personal data, this policy and the app's disclosures will be updated accordingly.

Data Retention and Deletion

The current version does not maintain user accounts or a server-side personal-data profile. Therefore, there is no account-based personal-data record to delete. Local app data can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage from Android settings.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate to the app's current functionality. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, permissions, SDKs, or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions about 8D WALL, contact the developer through the contact information listed on the app's Google Play store listing.
